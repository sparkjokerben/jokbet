一只住在屏幕角落的像素小东西 A pixel creature that lives in the corner of your screen

你打字，它掏出笔记本跟着敲；你点它一下，它挥手；双击，它比心。 Type and it takes out a laptop to type along. Click it and it waves; double-click and it makes a heart.

闲着的时候颠球、东张西望，没人理它就去睡觉。它还会数你按了多少键、点了多少下，数字就顶在头上。 Left alone it juggles, looks around, and eventually dozes off. It also counts your keys and clicks, and keeps the number over its head.

试试看Try it

在页面上敲几个键，它会掏出笔记本跟着敲。也可以单击、双击、按右键，或者把它拖到页面上的任何地方。 Type a few keys and it gets out a laptop to type along. Or click it, double-click it, right-click it, or drag it anywhere on the page.

它会什么What it does

  • 数你按了多少键（按每个键分开记）、左中右键点了多少下、滚了多少次、鼠标走了多少米，全部按天存。 Counts key presses (per key), left, right and middle clicks, scroll gestures and how far the mouse travelled — all kept per day.
  • 头顶一个数字：今天的总次数，或者此刻的手速；来源可以是键盘、鼠标或者两者合计。 One number over its head: today's total, or your speed right now, from the keyboard, the mouse, or both.
  • 你打字，它掏出笔记本一起敲；你点击、滚动，它有反应；光标到哪儿，它的眼睛跟到哪儿。 Type and it gets out a laptop and types along. It reacts to clicks and scrolling, and its eyes follow your cursor.
  • 闲着的时候做什么、单击和双击的反应，都可以自己选（颠球、东张西望、发呆、挥手、比心……）。 What it does when idle, and what a click or double-click gets you, are yours to pick (juggle, look around, breathe, wave, hearts…).
  • 统计窗口里有 7/30/90/365 天的趋势和一张键盘热力图（照着你的键盘画：Mac 上是 Apple 键盘，其他系统是 PC 键盘），可以导出 CSV。 A stats window with 7/30/90/365-day trends and a keyboard heatmap drawn on the board you have (an Apple one on a Mac, a PC one elsewhere), with CSV export.
  • 别的应用全屏或演示时它会自己让开；显示/隐藏和暂停计数都可以设全局快捷键。 It steps aside while another app is full screen or presenting, and global shortcuts can show or hide it and pause counting.
  • 中英双语，默认跟随系统，也可以自己选；到了自己设的里程碑会跳起来庆祝。 Speaks Chinese and English, following your system or your choice, and jumps for joy when you hit a milestone you set.
