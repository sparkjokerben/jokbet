屏幕角落里的像素桌宠，顺手数一数你按了多少键。 A pixel pet in the corner of your screen, counting your keys and clicks.

你打字，它掏出笔记本跟着敲；你点它，它会挥手、比心，或者踢一脚球。

You type, it gets out a laptop and types along. Poke it and it waves, makes a heart, or kicks a ball.

支持 macOS 11+、Windows 和 Linux（X11），以 GPL-3.0 开源 Runs on macOS 11+, Windows and Linux (X11). GPL-3.0.

    本次更新What changed

    安装包没有 Apple 或微软的签名，第一次打开时系统会拦截一次，按下面的步骤放行即可。

    The builds are not signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your system warns you once on the first launch. The steps below get you past it.

    它会什么What it does

    • 统计按键、左中右键点击、滚动手势和鼠标移动距离，按天保存 Counts key presses, left/right/middle clicks, scroll gestures and mouse travel, per day
    • 头顶一个数字：今天次数或实时手速，来源可选键盘、鼠标或合计 A number above its head: today's count or live speed, from the keyboard, the mouse, or both
    • 点它、双击、拖着它走都有反应，眼睛还会跟着光标转 Poking, double-clicking and dragging all get a reaction, and its eyes follow your cursor
    • 闲着的时候颠球、东张西望，或者只发呆——都可以自己选 When idle it juggles a ball, looks around, or just breathes — you pick
    • 统计面板有 7/30/90/365 天趋势和全尺寸键盘热力图，可以导出 CSV A stats window with 7/30/90/365-day trends and a full keyboard heatmap, plus CSV export
    • 界面中英双语（跟随系统语言），到了里程碑会跳起来庆祝 Speaks Chinese and English (following your system), and jumps for joy at milestones

    安装Install

    macOS

    1. 解压后把 jokerben-desktop-pet.app 拖进「应用程序」。Apple 芯片选 aarch64，Intel 选 x64。 Unzip it and move jokerben-desktop-pet.app to Applications. Take aarch64 for Apple silicon, x64 for Intel.
    2. 首次打开会被拦下：到「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」点「仍要打开」。（macOS 15 起右键「打开」已经不管用。）或者运行： The first launch is blocked: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. (Right-click → Open stopped working in macOS 15.) Or run: 
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/jokerben-desktop-pet.app
    3. 按提示授予「输入监控」权限。没有它，桌宠会一脸问号，也统计不到任何操作。更新后如果不计数了，在「输入监控」里删掉旧条目再重新添加。 Allow Input Monitoring when asked. Without it the pet shows a confused face and counts nothing. If counting stops after an update, remove the old entry from Input Monitoring and add the app again.

    Windows

    运行安装程序。SmartScreen 提示「Windows 已保护你的电脑」时，点「更多信息 → 仍要运行」。部分杀毒软件会把监听键盘的程序当成可疑程序；本程序只做计数。 Run the installer. If SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway. Some antivirus tools flag anything that listens to the keyboard; this app only counts.

    Linux (X11)

    用 AppImage（支持自动更新）或 deb 包。只支持 X11：Wayland 不允许程序监听全局输入。透明窗口需要合成器，托盘菜单需要 AppIndicator。 Use the AppImage (it self-updates) or the .deb. X11 only — Wayland does not let apps listen to global input. A compositor is needed for the transparent window, and an AppIndicator for the tray menu.

    隐私Privacy

    只保存计数：每个键按了几次、点击、滚动手势和鼠标移动距离。不记录输入内容，也不记录按键顺序。所有数据只存在本机。

    Only counts are stored: how many times each key was pressed, clicks, scroll gestures, and how far the mouse moved. It never records what you type or the order of your keys, and all data stays on your machine.

    数据在 ~/Library/Application Support/io.github.sparkjokerben.jokerben-desktop-pet/（Windows 是 %APPDATA%\\ 下、Linux 是 ~/.local/share/ 下同名目录）。

    It lives in ~/Library/Application Support/io.github.sparkjokerben.jokerben-desktop-pet/ (the same folder under %APPDATA%\\ on Windows and ~/.local/share/ on Linux).

    已知限制Known limitations

    • macOS：开了「安全键盘输入」时（终端设置或密码框里），按键对所有程序都不可见，桌宠会戴上眼罩。 macOS: keys typed while Secure Keyboard Entry is on are hidden from every app, including this one. The pet puts on a blindfold.
    • Windows：管理员权限窗口里的输入看不到（除非桌宠也以管理员运行）。桌宠会留在启动时的那个虚拟桌面。 Windows: input going to administrator windows is not seen unless the pet runs as administrator too. The pet stays on the virtual desktop it started on.
    • Linux：只支持 X11。 Linux: X11 only.
    • 桌宠会在全屏应用上面，挡事的时候从托盘菜单里先隐藏。 The pet stays on top of fullscreen apps too; hide it from the tray menu when it gets in the way.
